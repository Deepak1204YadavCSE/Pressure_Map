Responsible Use Guidelines & System Principles

A mandatory guide for health administrators, supervisors, and data evaluators.
🔍

1. What a Flag Means

A prompt to review, not proof of wrongdoing. A system flag (whether an impossible number, an unfair target, or a volume surge) is simply an automated invitation for supportive inquiry. It signals that recorded outputs do not align with baseline demographic projections, calling for an investigation into definitions, logistics, or administrative target-setting.

⚠️

2. What This Tool Cannot Tell

Numerical reporting models operate on simplified tallies and cannot capture ground-level operational realities. Specifically, this tool cannot detect:

  • Intensified Campaigns & Catch-Up Drives: Pulses of national immunization days, special outreach camps, or seasonal drives that rightfully elevate service volume.
  • Cross-Boundary Service Delivery: Beneficiaries travelling across village, block, or district borders to access higher-quality or more accessible healthcare facilities.
  • Data Corrections & Backlog Entries: Delayed data entry due to connectivity outages or batch reconciliation following resolve of supply stock-outs.
  • Denominator & Definition Mismatches: Outdated census estimates, migrant population shifts, or conflicting cohort definitions between doses.
🛑

3. What This Tool Never Does

Pressure Map is engineered with hard technical constraints to prevent surveillance and punitive enforcement:

  • No Worker Names or Staff IDs: All inputs and storage structures enforce a strict allowlist. Personal identifiers are rejected at the API boundary.
  • No Individual Worker Rankings or League Tables: Healthcare performance is evaluated at the aggregate catchment level, preventing competitive shaming.
  • No Blame Scores or Punishment Recommendations: All findings recommend system adjustments (revising targets, verifying census counts, providing transport), never disciplinary action against workers.
🔒

4. Privacy by Design

Privacy protection is embedded into the core logic rather than added as a superficial feature:

  • Group Totals Only: Records represent collective health facility tallies, eliminating patient or worker re-identification risks.
  • Small-Group Suppression (< 30 Eligible): Any demographic group with fewer than 30 individuals is masked in tables, omitted from maps, and strictly excluded from CSV exports and chart totals.
  • Worker Voice Protection (≥ 5 Reports Required): Feedback regarding systemic barriers is visible only when at least 5 workers have independently reported issues in that catchment area, ensuring anonymity.
  • Synthetic Demonstration Data: All demo data, coordinates, and metrics are synthetic to prevent disclosure of actual operational health records.
🤝

5. How Administrators Should Use Findings

When an administrative review reveals an unfair target or reporting gap, leadership should take corrective system actions:

  • Revise Unrealistic Targets: Adjust quotas downward to reflect true local demographic cohort counts using the suggested fair target range.
  • Verify Denominators & Boundaries: Commission updated micro-planning surveys to rectify outdated or inaccurate village census lists.
  • Add Operational Resources: Deploy supplementary vaccine carriers, cold chain technicians, or mobile transport vans to high-strain blocks.
  • Support Frontline Teams: Replace punitive target reviews with supportive supervisory visits and empathetic problem-solving.
