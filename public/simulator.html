Operational Capacity Caveat:
A target is never feasible from demographic population alone. Even if a target does not exceed the census denominator, local feasibility depends fundamentally on cold chain refrigeration capacity, transport availability, syringe/vial inventory, session scheduling, and frontline health worker staffing ratios.
